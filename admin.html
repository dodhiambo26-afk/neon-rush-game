Administrator support

NEON RUSH administration guide

• Tournament access and eligibility are controlled by the backend.

• Weekly tournament entry is KES 25.

• Monthly premium membership costs 300 Telegram Stars.

• Administrators can grant complimentary weekly or monthly access to registered players.

• Check Stars payment records before resolving payment disputes.

• Monthly membership expires at the end of its calendar month.

• Revoking membership removes the player's monthly access.

• Use player moderation carefully. Ban only when necessary.

• Review player performance, game history and tournament records when investigating issues.

• Keep your Telegram bot token, administrator credentials and Render secrets private.
